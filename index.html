About Rezonate

People-centred design.
Real-world impact.

At Rezonate, we design impactful services that put innovation and people at the heart. Our aim is to build services that fulfil the needs of their users — creating simplicity amongst complexity.

We build lasting relationships with our clients, working collaboratively to build services that work. From the first discovery session to final delivery, we're with you every step of the way.

Led by Zain Raza, an experienced senior service designer with deep expertise across public and private sectors. Zain uses the trusted double-diamond design methodology, combining UX research, service design, and strategic thinking to deliver outcomes that matter.

We thrive on complex services — connecting the dots, creating clarity from ambiguity, and converting insights into tangible ideas that deliver genuine value.
